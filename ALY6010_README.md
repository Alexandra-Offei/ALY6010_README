# ALY 6010 — Foundations of Analytics

**Northeastern University · Roux Institute**  
**Course:** ALY 6010 Foundations of Analytics  
**Language:** R · ggplot2 · R Markdown

---

## Overview

Applied statistical analysis and data wrangling in R across diverse real-world datasets, culminating in a regression-based real estate price prediction model on 7,701 property listings.

---

## Projects

### Module 1 — EDA: Car Dataset
**Dataset:** 637 vehicle records · 26 features

- Exploratory data analysis: structure, summary statistics, data types
- Missing value handling (1,194 NAs identified and documented)
- Distribution analysis of numerical and categorical variables

---

### Milestone 1 — Car Dataset Deep Dive
- Mean and median analysis of key vehicle attributes
- Central tendency and dispersion for performance metrics
- Visualisations of model year trends, manufacturer distributions

---

### Module 3 — BRFSS Health Survey Analysis
**Dataset:** 8,673 observations · 53 variables · CDC Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System

- Year column cleaning: string prefix removal with `str_replace`
- Income encoding: string ranges converted to numeric approximations
- Regional analysis of health risk factors across U.S. geographic zones
- Demographic breakdowns by income, age, and region

---

### Milestone 2 — Central Maine Power Weather Impact Analysis ⭐
**Dataset:** CMP outage data · 2019–2024 · 65 observations · 23 variables

- Analysis of how weather events affect Central Maine Power operations over 5 years
- New feature engineering: numeric wind speed extraction from string column
- Correlation between storm severity metrics and outage frequency/duration
- Time series patterns in weather-related service disruptions
- Local Maine dataset with direct community relevance

---

### Module 5 — Real Estate Price Prediction
**Dataset:** Redfin listings · key property attributes

- Correlation analysis: square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, lot size, days on market vs price
- Linear regression model for home price prediction
- Feature significance testing
- Residual analysis and model diagnostics

---

### Final Project — Real Estate Price Modelling ⭐
**Dataset:** 7,701 listings · 69 columns · Redfin + community health indicators

- Comprehensive multiple linear regression for home price prediction
- Feature selection from 69 variables: square footage, beds, baths, lot size, community indicators
- Model building, evaluation, and interpretation
- Identifying most influential property attributes for pricing
- Practical insights for buyers, real estate professionals, and market analysts

`R` `ggplot2` `lm()` `EDA` `Data cleaning` `Feature engineering` `Regression` `Real estate analytics` `Health data` `Time series`
